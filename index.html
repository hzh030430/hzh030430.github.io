Work in progress

Under reviewFirst author

Out of Step with Evidence

I study when tool-using agents report task progress that is not supported by the available evidence. I develop trace-based methods to diagnose mismatches between observations, actions, and completion claims, and to distinguish these from tool or execution-environment failures.

I lead the research design, implementation, experiments, analysis, and writing.
